How It Works

Secret: The private value known to the prover.

Public key: A value derived from the secret.

Commitment: A temporary value used to construct the proof.

Challenge: A value derived from the proof transcript.

Response: The prover's response used by the verifier.

Educational illustration only. This simplified demo uses a small finite group and does not provide production-grade security.
